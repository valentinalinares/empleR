# empleR: Tools for Analyzing Peru's National Employment Surveys (EPEN/EPE)

Provides tools to download, read, and analyze microdata from Peru's
national employment surveys: the Encuesta Permanente de Empleo Nacional
(EPEN, 2022-present) and its predecessor the Encuesta Permanente de
Empleo (EPE, until 2021), produced by Peru's Instituto Nacional de
Estadistica e Informatica (INEI) and distributed through its public
Microdata System <https://proyectos.inei.gob.pe/microdatos/>. Key
features include automatic harmonization of occupational classifiers
(CNO 2015 and CO-95), calculation of weighted labor market indicators
with optional experimental survey-design inference, EPEN
departmental-domain maps, and integration with an AI occupational
exposure index.
