# empleR: Tools for Analyzing Peru's National Employment Surveys (EPEN/EPE)

Provides tools to download, read, and analyze microdata from Peru's
national employment surveys: the Encuesta Permanente de Empleo Nacional
(EPEN, 2022-present) and its predecessor the Encuesta Permanente de
Empleo (EPE, until 2021). Key features include automatic harmonization
of occupational classifiers (CNO 2015 and CO-95), calculation of labor
market indicators using complex survey design, regional visualization
via interactive maps, and integration with an AI occupational exposure
index.
